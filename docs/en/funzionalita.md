# Functional catalogue

VideoTrack 1.6.29 supports three video adapters; trusted-form automatic duration suggestion from available YouTube, Vimeo and local metadata; watched-segment tracking; progress, completion and gradebook; reactions and reaction presets; personal notes and private bookmarks; WebVTT captions/transcript/chapters; poster and accessible custom controls; resume, replay and seek/rate policies; optional Forum posting; instance, course and teacher reports; same-video cross-course Analytics; CSV/Excel/ODS and individual CSV exports; integrity/focus controls; versioned acknowledgements; Privacy API; retention; backup/restore; reset and Moodle events.

Every optional collection feature is disabled by default or explicitly configured. See the numbered documents for contracts and limitations.
